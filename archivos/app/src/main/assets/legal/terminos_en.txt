## Risks and backups
- Copying, moving, deleting, renaming and compressing really change your files. Check before confirming.
- "Delete forever" can't be undone. The recycle bin keeps deleted items for only 30 days.
- If you forget the Vault's PIN, nobody can recover what's inside, not even us. Don't delete the ".Boveda-segura" folder either.
- If you turn on auto-wipe in the Vault settings and someone enters a wrong PIN 7 times in a row, everything in the Vault is deleted forever. Nobody can recover it, not even us.
- An APK signed with a different key won't install over the original: you have to uninstall the original, which deletes its data. Signing an APK doesn't make it safe: only install what you trust.
- Back up what matters to you. We don't keep copies of your files.
